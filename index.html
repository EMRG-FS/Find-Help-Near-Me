Find Help Near Me

Choose your college and the type of help you want. You will get links to resources at your college, in your county, and California-wide.

New to this tool? Watch the short how-to video first. It shows each step, from choosing your college to saving your results.

Watch the how-to video (opens in a new tab)

Before you start: This tool does not apply for anything, does not save anything, and cannot tell you whether you qualify for any program or resource. Instead, it points you to official websites, so you can follow up for more information and contact them for help. Asking for help is a good first step.

Step 1: Choose your college

If your college is not listed, choose the last choice. You will still get links for your county and California.

Step 2: Check your county

Choosing a college fills in its county. You can change it. If you are not sure, choose the last choice.

Step 3: Choose the help you want

Choose all that apply
