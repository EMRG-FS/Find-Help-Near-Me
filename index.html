Find Help Near Me

Choose your college and the kinds of help you want. You will get links to your college, your county, and California.

Before you start: This tool does not apply for anything, does not save anything, and cannot tell you whether you qualify for any program. It points you to official websites so you can ask. Asking for help is a good first step.

Step 1: Choose your college

If your college is not listed, choose the last choice. You will still get links for your county and California.

Step 2: Check your county

Choosing a college fills in its county. You can change it. If you are not sure, choose the last choice.

Step 3: Choose the help you want
